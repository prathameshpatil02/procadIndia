Material in context

1.2312

Optimised for material removal

Higher sulphur promotes easy, self-breaking chips and fast machining. It is most useful where speed matters more than cosmetic cavity finish; sulphide inclusions reduce polishability and make welding repairs more difficult.

1.2738

Built for high-fidelity molding

Nickel improves through-hardening, polishability and dimensional stability in larger blocks. It is a strong choice when part finish, abrasive materials or longer mold life demand more from the steel.

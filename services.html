Plastic injection mold making

Art meets
repeatability.

Precision molds engineered for the actual production environment. We support new builds, modification work, troubleshooting and recovery of tooling issues.

  • CAD/CAM, CNC, EDM and inspection-led workflow
  • Complex mold engineering and production troubleshooting
  • Plastic molded components across a variety of resins
03

Rapid / soft / pilot tooling

Test fast.
Launch smarter.

When time-to-market is critical, rapid tools help prove functionality, manufacturability, reliability and color selection before a large tooling commitment.

  • Typical 100 × 100 mm tools in 7–15 days
  • Soft tooling up to 500 × 500 × 200 mm in around 30 days
  • Pilot tooling in P20, 1.2311 or 1.2738 for initial production lots

  • Delrin, Teflon, PVC, nylon and silicone elastomers
  • Machine spares including POM gears and swash plates

Our story / Since 2006

Design thinking.
Toolroom discipline.

PS Toolings began in October 2006 as Pro CADCAM Services, providing 3D design, product design, reverse engineering, CAM programming up to five axes, 2D drafting, CNC machining and software training.

Today, PS Toolings is a precision mold maker and supplier of injection-molded, CNC-machined components. We take on complex tooling challenges—new builds, modification work, production recovery and the engineering decisions that make the difference between an attractive model and a reliable product.

Our work is led by Prakash Shetty, a mechanical engineer whose career began in the Godrej & Boyce toolroom and aerospace component manufacturing. That practical foundation is what keeps every PS Toolings solution grounded in manufacturability.
